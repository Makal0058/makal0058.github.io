About

I am a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at UESTC. My research focuses on machine learning, reinforcement learning, latent reasoning, and reliable reasoning in large language models.

Machine Learning Reinforcement Learning Latent Reasoning Large Language Models Reasoning Faithfulness

Research

Latent Reasoning

I am exploring neural architectures that perform multi-step internal reasoning in latent space rather than generating language tokens at every step, with an emphasis on multi-candidate reasoning, memory, communication, and selective information reuse.

Faithfulness of LLM Reasoning

I study whether large language models truly follow task structure during reasoning, rather than relying on positional or candidate shortcuts.

Selected Projects

Graph-Intervention Faithfulness (GIF)

Research project

A framework for testing whether language models follow graph structure under controlled interventions, with diagnostics at both answer and reasoning-path levels.

Latent Reasoning Engine

Ongoing

Exploring Transformer-based internal reasoning with multiple latent candidates and source-aware memory, aiming to reason without requiring token-level chain-of-thought generation.
